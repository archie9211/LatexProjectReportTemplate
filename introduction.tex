In recent years road accidents have become very common in cities, whether it be a highway or the main city areas. In big cities the frequency of such road accidents has increased due to many modes of transportation , traffic lights not working or the roads becoming narrow and overcrowded. Such accidents result in life and property loss.  

Keeping in mind such everyday road rage situations, we need helping hands in assisting us to drive safely. Even though there are measures like safety belts and air bags in all of the cars today but getting an alert about a possible accident is more helpful. Thus it has become important to detect the abnormal behavior of the vehicles driving around our vehicle in a quick and accurate manner. The assisting systems should be fast and reliable to detect any possible threat to our vehicle.

The proposed approach will be able to identify the abnormal behavior of the vehicles in the surroundings and cast a warning. Abnormal behavior here means if a vehicle suddenly changes its velocity and there could be a chance that the vehicle’s path could converge leading to road fatalities. This approach will help in vehicle detection and using properties of the surrounding vehicles that is their speed and the direction of movement it will do classification of vehicles.

Since this problem of vehicle movement detection needs to be solved in real time so an approach is needed which can process these properties of a moving vehicle in a faster and efficient way. While image processing the assistants should clearly be able to distinguish between the several objects on the road and be able to separate foreground and background to look at more eminent threats. The algorithms used in the proposed approach implements this separation much accurately. The assistants should be accustomed to all the region domains it may have to face during driving in any geographical location.

Deep learning and machine learning are emerging technologies in today’s world and have an immense use in computer vision and image processing. These techniques can be used to solve our problem specially fully convolution neural network and support vector machine. Using these techniques first a model is build and this model is trained to perform a particular task. Once training is done these models can perform the required task very accurately and efficiently.
