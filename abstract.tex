\begin{abstract}

Due to recent surge in road accidents due to negligent driving of one vehicle driver which costs loss in life and property there is a need for an efficient and quick method to detect abnormal driving in real time situations. In this report, a method is proposed which solves the slow detection of abnormal driving. With the help of  YOLO algorithm firstly, the vehicle is identified by extracting the region of interest(ROI) and providing parameters like height, width and center coordinates of ROI. Then Lucas-Kanade algorithm is used for optical flow analysis, after aggregating the vectors calculated earlier by simple averaging which provides a motion vector, the driving behaviour of a vehicle is classified as abnormal or normal. 
\end{abstract}
